Carambola

Launch Carambola from the Start Menu shortcut. The desktop shortcut is optional.
Close Carambola before installing an upgrade or uninstalling it. Setup and the
uninstaller do not terminate the app automatically.

User settings and diagnostics under %APPDATA%\Carambola are retained during
upgrades and uninstall. Remove that folder yourself only if you also want to
delete your Carambola user data.
